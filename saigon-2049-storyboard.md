# Sài Gòn 2049 — Storyboard (bản mặc định)

Định dạng: 6 cảnh, 16:9, ~40 giây, không nhân vật chính, âm thanh môi trường (không thoại, không nhạc).
Chủ đề: Sài Gòn tương lai ven sông, thích ứng khí hậu, chợ nổi hiện đại. Tông: xanh sáng, ấm hoàng hôn, không u tối.

| # | Cảnh | Thời lượng | Prompt (tiếng Anh, dùng cho Seedance 2.5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bình minh trên sông | 6s | Wide aerial shot at sunrise over the Saigon River in 2049, a futuristic Ho Chi Minh City skyline with green terraced towers and vertical gardens, soft golden light, light mist on the water. Slow forward drone move. River ambience and distant city hum. |
| 2 | Chợ nổi tương lai | 6s | Floating market of 2049 on the Saigon River: solar-roofed wooden boats loaded with fruit and flowers, glowing holographic price signs, vendors in modern ao ba ba. Camera glides along the water at boat level. Water lapping, market chatter without clear words. |
| 3 | Phố trên cao | 5s | Elevated pedestrian skyways between green towers in Saigon 2049, silent electric trams and autonomous pods, banyan trees growing on rooftops, people walking with umbrellas in light rain. Smooth tracking shot. Soft rain and tram hum. |
| 4 | Ven kênh thích ứng khí hậu | 8s | Canal district of Saigon 2049 designed for high tides: raised promenades, flood gates, small ferries, cafes on stilts with lanterns lighting up at dusk. Camera slowly descends from rooftop to the water. Gentle water, distant bells. |
| 5 | Đêm đèn lồng | 7s | Saigon 2049 night, riverside plaza with lantern-like light installations, street food stalls with induction carts, drones carrying warm lights overhead, crowds gathering. Slow orbit around the plaza. Crowd murmur and soft ambience. |
| 6 | Toàn cảnh khép lại | 8s | Final wide shot from the river at blue hour: Saigon 2049 skyline reflected in calm water, a sampan with a small light crossing the frame in the foreground, city lights turning on gradually. Slow pull-back. River ambience fading. |

## Ước tính chi phí (Seedance 2.5, 720p, ~1,5 credit/giây)
Tổng 40s ≈ 60 credit. Cần ảnh khung đầu cho mỗi cảnh (ảnh GPT Image 2.5 1K medium: dùng lượt miễn phí, còn 8).
